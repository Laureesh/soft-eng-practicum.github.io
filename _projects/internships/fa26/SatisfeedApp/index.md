This Fall 2026 internship combines and continues two earlier projects: Thanh Tran’s [Satisfeed: Data Analytics App](https://soft-eng-practicum.github.io/projects/internships/sp25/SatisfeedDataAnalyticsApp/index.html) from Spring 2025 and the [Satisfeed: GeoAnalytics App](https://soft-eng-practicum.github.io/projects/sd2/fa25/SatisfeedGwinnettAnalyticsApp/index.html), developed in Fall 2025 by “Let ’Em Cook”—Dylan Long, Ewura Ama Awere, and Sam Keller.

My Fall 2026 internship combines features from these projects and continues
their development. Laureesh Volmar’s contributions include improving dashboard and map features, assisting with data modeling, and developing role-based access and Manage Admins features. He also improves API validation and error handling, troubleshoots issues, and merges SD2 team contributions while resolving conflicts. His work includes configuring Docker, Nginx, and HTTPS, preparing the application for AWS deployment, and documenting setup instructions, progress, and remaining work.
